# Data Classification Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines the firm's data sensitivity tiers and the handling rules that
apply to each tier, so that controls are proportionate to the risk of the data.

## Classification Tiers

Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted. Client
personal data and regulated records are, at minimum, Confidential. Material
non-public information is Restricted.

## Handling Requirements

Confidential and Restricted data must be encrypted in transit and at rest.
Encryption keys are managed under the [[Cryptographic Key Management Standard]].
Access to Restricted data follows the [[Access Control Policy]].

## Retention and Disposal

Classified records are retained and disposed of in accordance with the
[[Data Retention Policy]].
