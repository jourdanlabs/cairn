# Data Retention Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines how long the firm retains client records and regulated data,
and how such records are disposed of at the end of their lifecycle. It applies to
all client account documentation across the firm's lines of business.

## Client Record Retention

Client account records, transaction histories, and Know Your Customer
documentation must be retained for a minimum of **seven (7) years** following the
closure of the client relationship. Retention covers onboarding files,
suitability assessments, instructions, and correspondence. Records are held in the
approved records repository and are disposed of only after the retention period
has lapsed and any legal hold has been cleared.

## Legal Hold

When records are subject to litigation, regulatory inquiry, or investigation, a
legal hold suspends all disposal. Legal hold is released only by the Legal
function in writing.

## Disposal

At the end of the retention period, records are destroyed using approved secure
methods, and destruction is logged.
