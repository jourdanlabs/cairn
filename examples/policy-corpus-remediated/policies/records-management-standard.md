# Records Management Standard

## Purpose

This standard specifies how the firm classifies, stores, and retains client
records and regulated data by record type, and how such records are disposed of
at the end of their lifecycle. It supports the firm's records obligations across
all lines of business.

## Client Record Retention

Retention *periods* for client records are not restated in this standard. They are
defined authoritatively in the [[Data Retention Policy]], which sets the firm-wide
minimum retention for client account records, transaction histories, and Know Your
Customer documentation. This standard governs *how* those records are classified,
stored, and disposed of once the retention period defined there has elapsed and any
legal hold has been cleared. Where this standard and the Data Retention Policy
appear to differ, the Data Retention Policy governs.

## Storage

Records are stored in the approved repository with access restricted to
authorized personnel. Storage tiers are aligned to the sensitivity of the record.

## Disposal

At the end of the retention period, records are destroyed using approved secure
methods, and destruction is logged.

## Related Controls

Firm-wide retention obligations are set by the [[Data Retention Policy]].
