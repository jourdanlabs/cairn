# Access Control Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines how logical access to firm systems and client data is
granted, reviewed, and revoked. It implements the access-control requirements of
the [[Information Security Policy]].

## Principle of Least Privilege

Access is granted on a least-privilege, need-to-know basis. Entitlements are tied
to documented job roles. Privileged and administrative accounts are restricted,
individually attributable, and subject to enhanced monitoring.

## Authentication

Multi-factor authentication is required for all remote access and for all access
to systems holding client or regulated data. Shared credentials are prohibited.
Passwords must meet the firm's complexity and rotation standards.

## Access Reviews

Entitlement recertification is performed quarterly by system owners. Access for
transferred or terminated personnel is revoked within 24 hours. Any access that
cannot be attributed to an active role is removed.

## Related Controls

Access changes are executed through the [[Change Management Policy]]. Suspected
credential compromise is escalated under the [[Incident Response Policy]].
