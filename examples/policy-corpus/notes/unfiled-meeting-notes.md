# Unfiled Compliance Sync Notes

Rough notes from the monthly compliance sync that were never filed into the
control library or linked to any policy. Attendees discussed upcoming examination
readiness, a backlog of vendor reassessments, and training completion rates across
the front office. Nobody was assigned to convert these notes into policy updates,
so they sit here unreferenced and unreachable from the rest of the library.
