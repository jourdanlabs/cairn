# Information Security Policy

## Purpose and Scope

This Information Security Policy establishes the firm's overarching requirements
for protecting the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of client data,
firm systems, and regulated records. It applies to all employees, contractors,
and third parties that access firm information assets.

This policy sits at the top of the control library and is implemented through the
subordinate policies and standards referenced below.

## Governance and Responsibilities

The Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) owns this policy and the security
program. Business unit heads are accountable for compliance within their units.
Independent assurance is provided by Internal Audit on an annual basis.

## Supporting Policies

Personnel and system access are governed by the [[Access Control Policy]].
Security events and breaches are handled under the [[Incident Response Policy]].
Data sensitivity tiers and handling rules are defined in the
[[Data Classification Policy]]. Retention and disposal obligations are set by the
[[Data Retention Policy]]. System and configuration changes follow the
[[Change Management Policy]], and external providers are assessed under the
[[Vendor Risk Management Policy]].

## Exceptions

Any exception to this policy requires documented CISO approval, a compensating
control, and a defined expiry date. Exceptions are reviewed quarterly.
