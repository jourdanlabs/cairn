# Incident Response Policy

## Purpose

This policy establishes how the firm detects, triages, contains, and reports
information-security incidents, including suspected breaches of client data.

## Severity Classification

Incidents are classified from SEV-1 (material impact to clients or regulated
data) through SEV-4 (negligible impact). Classification drives escalation
timelines and notification obligations.

## Response Phases

The response lifecycle is: detection, triage, containment, eradication,
recovery, and post-incident review. Each phase has defined owners and evidence
requirements. A lessons-learned review is mandatory for every SEV-1 and SEV-2.

## Regulatory Notification

Where an incident involves personal or regulated client data, the Compliance and
Legal functions determine regulatory notification obligations and timelines.
Incidents implicating suspicious financial activity are also referred under the
[[AML and KYC Program Policy]].

## Related Controls

Account and credential compromise is contained using the [[Access Control Policy]].
Incidents originating at a service provider are coordinated with the
[[Vendor Risk Management Policy]].
