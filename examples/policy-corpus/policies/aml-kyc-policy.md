# AML and KYC Program Policy

## Purpose

This policy sets out the firm's Anti-Money-Laundering (AML) and Know-Your-Customer
(KYC) program, consistent with applicable regulatory obligations. It governs
client due diligence, ongoing monitoring, and suspicious-activity reporting.

## Customer Due Diligence

At onboarding, the firm verifies client identity, beneficial ownership, and the
expected nature of the relationship. Enhanced due diligence applies to higher-risk
clients, including politically exposed persons.

## Ongoing Monitoring

Client activity is monitored against expected behavior. Material deviations are
escalated for review. Where activity is deemed suspicious, a report is filed with
the relevant authority within the required timeframe.

## Recordkeeping

KYC documentation and transaction records are retained in accordance with the
[[Data Retention Policy]] and the [[Records Management Standard]]. These records
must be retrievable to support regulatory examination.

## Related Controls

Suspicious activity that also constitutes a security incident is coordinated with
the [[Incident Response Policy]].
