# Vendor Risk Management Policy

## Purpose

This policy governs how the firm identifies, assesses, and monitors risk arising
from third-party service providers, including those that process client or
regulated data on the firm's behalf.

## Due Diligence

Before onboarding, each vendor is risk-tiered based on data sensitivity and
criticality. Tier 1 vendors undergo full security due diligence, including review
of their access controls and incident-handling capability. Findings must be
remediated or formally accepted before contract signature.

## Contractual Controls

Contracts with vendors that handle client data must include confidentiality,
breach-notification, audit-rights, and data-return-or-destruction clauses. Vendor
data-handling must be consistent with the firm's [[Data Retention Policy]].

## Ongoing Monitoring

Tier 1 vendors are reassessed annually. Access granted to vendor personnel is
provisioned and reviewed under the [[Access Control Policy]]. Vendor-originated
security events are handled through the [[Incident Response Policy]].

## Governance

This policy implements the third-party requirements of the
[[Information Security Policy]].
