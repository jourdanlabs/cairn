# Ordinance 2026-04 — Short-term rental annual cap amendment

*(Synthetic demo document — the Town of Alder Creek and every person and event are fictional.)*

An ordinance of the Town of Alder Creek amending § 5-210 of the Alder Creek Municipal Code to reduce the annual cap on short-term rental days.

## Section 1 — Findings

The Town Council finds that short-term rental activity has grown faster than the housing stock, and that the existing annual cap no longer serves the purposes stated when Ordinance 2019-07 was adopted.

## Section 2 — Amendment of § 5-210

Subsection (b) of § 5-210 of the Alder Creek Municipal Code ("Short-term rentals — annual cap") is amended to read in full:

> (b) A dwelling unit may be offered or rented as a short-term rental for no more than ninety (90) days in any calendar year. Each rental day counts against the annual cap, and no permit holder may exceed the cap across booking platforms.

All other provisions of § 5-210 remain unchanged.

## Section 3 — Effective date

This ordinance takes effect April 15, 2026. Any provision of the codified Municipal Code inconsistent with this ordinance is superseded as of that date.
