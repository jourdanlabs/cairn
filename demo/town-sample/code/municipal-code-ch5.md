## § 5-200 Definitions

"Short-term rental" means a dwelling unit, or any room within one, offered for rent for fewer than thirty (30) consecutive days. "Booking platform" means any service through which a short-term rental is offered or reserved.

## § 5-210 Short-term rentals — annual cap

(a) No dwelling unit may be offered as a short-term rental without a current short-term rental permit issued by the town clerk.

(b) A dwelling unit may be offered or rented as a short-term rental for no more than one hundred twenty (120) days in any calendar year. Each rental day counts against the annual cap, and no permit holder may exceed the cap across booking platforms.

(c) A violation of this section is a civil infraction of $250 per rental day beyond the cap.

## § 5-220 Permit and fee

The short-term rental permit fee is set by the consolidated fee schedule adopted by resolution (see Resolution 2019-33). Permits are issued for the calendar year and are not transferable between dwelling units.
