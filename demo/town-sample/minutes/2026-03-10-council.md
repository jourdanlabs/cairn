# Town Council minutes — regular meeting, March 10, 2026 (excerpt)

*(Synthetic demo document — the Town of Alder Creek and every person and event are fictional.)*

Mayor Elena Reyes called the meeting to order at 6:02 p.m. Present: Council Members Dara Okonkwo, Tomás Ferreira, June Albrecht, Hollis Vann, and Pete Skoglund. Town Clerk Marisol Winters recorded the minutes.

## Agenda item 6 — Second reading, Ordinance 2026-04 (short-term rental cap)

Council Member Okonkwo moved to adopt Ordinance 2026-04, amending § 5-210(b) of the Municipal Code to reduce the short-term rental annual cap to ninety (90) days per calendar year. Council Member Ferreira seconded.

Council Member Okonkwo said complaint volume from the Mill District had doubled since 2024 and that the cap adopted under Ordinance 2019-07 no longer fit the housing market. Council Member Skoglund objected that the reduction penalizes permit holders who booked in reliance on the existing cap.

Roll-call vote: Okonkwo — aye; Ferreira — aye; Albrecht — aye; Vann — aye; Skoglund — nay. Motion carried 4–1. Ordinance 2026-04 adopted, effective April 15, 2026.

## Agenda item 7 — Clerk's report

Clerk Winters reported that adopted ordinances would be transmitted to the codifier in the April batch.

Meeting adjourned at 7:41 p.m.
