# Town Council minutes — regular meeting, May 12, 2026 (excerpt)

*(Synthetic demo document — the Town of Alder Creek and every person and event are fictional.)*

Mayor Elena Reyes called the meeting to order at 6:04 p.m. Present: Council Members Dara Okonkwo, Tomás Ferreira, June Albrecht, Hollis Vann, and Pete Skoglund. Town Clerk Marisol Winters recorded the minutes.

## Agenda item 4 — Short-term rental enforcement report

The code enforcement officer reported that two permit holders contested notices of violation by citing the published Municipal Code, which still shows the annual cap that Ordinance 2026-04 amended. Council Member Okonkwo asked which figure staff are enforcing; the town attorney advised that the adopted ordinance controls over the codified text.

Council Member Okonkwo moved to direct the town clerk to expedite codification of Ordinance 2026-04 with the codifier no later than June 30, 2026, and to direct staff to mail every current short-term rental permit holder a notice of the ninety (90) day annual cap by May 29, 2026. Council Member Albrecht seconded.

Roll-call vote: Okonkwo — aye; Ferreira — aye; Albrecht — aye; Vann — aye; Skoglund — aye. Motion carried 5–0.

Meeting adjourned at 7:12 p.m.
