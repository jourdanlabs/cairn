# SOP-300-12 — Unit 300 Amine Contactor V-301 Startup (Rev 4)

*(Synthetic demo document — Gulf Coast Gas Processing LLC is fictional.)*

**Facility:** Gulf Coast Gas Processing LLC, Unit 300 Amine Treating.
**Procedure:** SOP-300-12, Revision 4. Effective November 3, 2024. Last annual review September 2, 2025.
**Covers:** startup of amine contactor V-301 from a purged, depressured state.

## 1. Prerequisites

- Lean amine (MDEA, 45–50 wt%) available at the charge pump suction.
- PSV-3010 in service; block valves car-sealed open.
- Shift supervisor authorization recorded in the shift log.

## 2. Operating limits

| Parameter | Limit |
|---|---|
| Contactor operating pressure | 915 psig normal; do not exceed 985 psig |
| Lean amine circulation before gas-in | 210 gpm minimum |
| Lean amine temperature | 110 °F minimum, and at least 10 °F above inlet gas temperature |
| Vent oxygen before gas-in | below 1.0 vol% O2 |

## 3. Nitrogen purge

3.1 Purge the amine contactor V-301 and associated piping with nitrogen for a minimum of thirty (30) minutes before admitting sour gas.
3.2 Verify oxygen concentration at the vent is below 1.0 vol% O2 before proceeding.
3.3 Record purge start and end times in the shift log.

## 4. Establish circulation and gas-in

4.1 Establish lean amine circulation at 210 gpm minimum and confirm level in the bottom of V-301 at 50–60%.
4.2 Raise contactor pressure with sweet fuel gas in steps of no more than 100 psi.
4.3 Admit sour gas slowly and line out at 915 psig.
