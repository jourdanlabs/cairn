# MOC-2026-018 — Reduced nitrogen purge duration for V-301 startup

*(Synthetic demo document — Gulf Coast Gas Processing LLC is fictional.)*

**Facility:** Gulf Coast Gas Processing LLC, Unit 300 Amine Treating.
**Type:** Permanent change. **Status:** Approved April 18, 2026; effective April 22, 2026.

## Change description

Reduce the nitrogen purge duration in SOP-300-12 Section 3: purge the amine contactor V-301 and associated piping with nitrogen for a minimum of fifteen (15) minutes before admitting sour gas. Verify oxygen concentration at the vent is below 1.0 vol% O2 before proceeding. All other requirements of SOP-300-12 Section 3 are unchanged.

## Technical basis

The in-line oxygen analyzer AT-3012 (installed March 2026) provides continuous vent O2 measurement during purge. Data from eleven startups shows vent O2 below 1.0 vol% within nine (9) minutes at standard nitrogen flow; fifteen (15) minutes with continuous analyzer confirmation provides margin equivalent to the prior time-based practice.
