# Finance FAQ — common metric questions

*(Synthetic demo document — Brightpath Software Inc. is fictional.)*

## How do we calculate ARR?

ARR is the sum of active subscription contract MRR multiplied by twelve, measured as of the last calendar day of the month. ARR includes usage overages annualized on a trailing three-month average, plus one-time professional services recognized in the period. This is the number Finance reports to the exec team.

*(Copied from the RevOps definition page in 2024 — check with RevOps before relying on the overage treatment.)*

## What about NRR?

NRR compares this month's cohort MRR to the same cohort a year ago. Expansion and churn both count; new customers don't. See RevOps for the cohort extract.

## Who owns these numbers?

Historically Finance produced ARR for board reporting, with RevOps providing the subscription extract. Questions go to the finance-metrics channel.
