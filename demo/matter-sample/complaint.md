# Complaint — Meridian Fabrication LLC v. Copperline Industrial Supply Co.

*(Synthetic demo document — all parties and events are fictional.)*

## Parties

1. Plaintiff Meridian Fabrication LLC ("Meridian") is a Texas limited liability company with its principal place of business in Pasadena, Texas. Meridian is represented by Whitlock & Chen LLP.
2. Defendant Copperline Industrial Supply Co. ("Copperline") is a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business in Tulsa, Oklahoma. Copperline is represented by Garza Findlay PC.

## Factual allegations

3. On January 9, 2026, Meridian issued Purchase Order 4471 to Copperline for 12,000 galvanized steel mounting brackets, grade A572-50, at a total price of $186,000, for delivery no later than March 6, 2026.
4. The brackets were required for Meridian's contract with a petrochemical facility expansion, a fact Copperline knew at the time of contracting.
5. Copperline delivered the shipment on March 9, 2026 — three days late.
6. Upon inspection, a substantial portion of the delivered brackets were non-conforming: coating thickness below the specified 3.9 mils and evidence of white rust on multiple pallets.
7. Meridian's operations manager, Daniel Hargrove, documented the non-conformities and provided written notice of rejection to Copperline on March 16, 2026.
8. Copperline refused to replace the non-conforming brackets or refund the purchase price.

## Claims

9. **Count I — Breach of contract.** Copperline breached PO 4471 by late delivery and by tendering non-conforming goods.
10. **Count II — Breach of express warranty.** The goods failed to conform to the express specifications in PO 4471.

Meridian seeks damages of not less than $412,000, including cover costs and delay damages on the downstream contract.
