# Deposition of Daniel Hargrove (excerpt)

*(Synthetic demo document — all parties and events are fictional.)*

Daniel Hargrove, called as a witness, testified as follows. Mr. Hargrove is the operations manager at Meridian Fabrication LLC and has held that role since 2021. He reports to Meridian's VP of Projects, Carla Voss.

**Q.** When did the Copperline shipment arrive at your facility?
**A.** March 9. A Monday. I signed the bill of lading myself.

**Q.** And when did you first inspect the brackets?
**A.** I opened the crates and inspected the shipment on March 12. I pulled brackets from four different pallets and measured coating thickness on each. Most of what I pulled read between 3.2 and 3.6 mils. The spec was 3.9.

**Q.** Did you photograph what you found?
**A.** Yes, on the 12th. Those are the photos we produced.

**Q.** Where were the crates stored between delivery on March 9 and your inspection?
**A.** In the staging yard. Covered. We always tarp incoming steel.

**Q.** You're certain the crates were tarped?
**A.** That's our standard procedure. I didn't personally check the tarps that week, but that's the procedure.

**Q.** And the white rust — when did you first observe it?
**A.** During my March 12 inspection. Pallets two and five, mostly.
