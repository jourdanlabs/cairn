The record hall

A city chatbot once told business owners to break the law, confidently. The public record deserves better: a system that answers only from the adopted record, cites what it answers, and refuses what was never adopted. And because codification always lags, CAIRN finds where your published code disagrees with your adopted law — before a resident's lawyer does.

How this room works

Four acts, each performed live by the box behind this page — nothing is mocked, and every response's raw JSON is one toggle away. Complete an act to open the next. The finale is yours to cause: a refusal, sealed to a hash-chained ledger, that you then verify with your own hands.
