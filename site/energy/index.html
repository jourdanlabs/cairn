The control room

Every year a named person signs a certification that the operating procedures are current and accurate. Then an MOC changes the purge time, the SOP never gets revised, and the crews drift — the exact finding investigators write after the worst day. CAIRN reads the controlled set the way an auditor would: a required action with no completion evidence is OPEN — never assumed done.
