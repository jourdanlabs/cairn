The gate that refuses

Lawyers keep getting sanctioned for citations that don't exist. CAIRN is architecturally incapable of citing a case that is not in the matter file — it refuses, on the record, with a hash-sealed receipt. And inside the file, it does the work of a tireless second chair: every fact on a witness card carries a verbatim quote, mechanically verified against the record. A pin cite, enforced by a machine.
